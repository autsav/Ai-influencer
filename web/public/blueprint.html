WHAT YOU'VE BEEN TOLD

The 5 Lies About AI Entrepreneurship

Lie 1: "You need to be a tech genius"

You don't. You need to know what you want. The AI handles the how.

Lie 2: "It replaces humans entirely"

It doesn't. It handles the stuff you hate — the repetitive, the administrative, the boring.

Lie 3: "Set it up once and forget it"

You can't. Every system needs tending. But 10 minutes of maintenance beats 10 hours of manual work.

Lie 4: "You need a huge budget"

Most of what you need is free or under $30/mo.

Lie 5: "Overnight success is real"

Consistency is unglamorous. But it's the only thing that compounds.

START HERE

Your First Automation
in 30 minutes

The Email triage automation:

Tools needed: Gmail + a free Make.com account

  1. Create a filter in Gmail that labels all inbound client emails as "LEAD" or "SUPPORT"
  2. In Make.com, create a scenario that:

- Triggers when a new email arrives

- Reads the subject line with AI

- Sends an auto-reply based on intent (qualified lead → booking link | support → help doc)

- Adds the contact to your CRM spreadsheet

Result: You're sleeping. Your inbox is sorting itself.

WHAT BREAKS & HOW TO SHIP

The 3 Mistakes That Kill Automations

Mistake 1: Automating chaos

If your process is messy, automating it just makes the mess faster.

Fix the process first. Automate second.

Mistake 2: Trying to automate everything

Start with ONE thing. The thing that takes the most time and the least thinking.

Usually: email triage, or invoice reminders, or social media scheduling.

Mistake 3: Setting it and forgetting it

Check your automations weekly for the first month.

Things change. Upstream apps update. Assumptions break.

Your 5-Day Setup Sprint

Day 1 — Audit

Write down everything you do manually that takes more than 5 minutes.

Day 2 — Pick one

Choose the task that costs you the most time and the least brain power.

This is your first automation.

Day 3 — Learn Make.com

Create a free account. Watch 2 of their template videos.

You don't need to understand everything — just enough to copy a template.

Day 4 — Build it

Follow a template for your specific task.

It won't be perfect. It doesn't need to be.

Day 5 — Test and launch

Run it manually 3 times. Fix what breaks.

Then turn it on and let it run.
