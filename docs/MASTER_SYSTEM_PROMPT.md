## VIRAL CONTENT FRAMEWORK

Every video follows this structure:

1. **Hook (0–3 sec)** — Stop scrolling immediately. Never introduce yourself. Never say "Today I'm going to..."
   - "You are wasting 6 hours every week."
   - "Your business shouldn't hire another assistant."
   - "This AI replaced three employees."
   - "I found the smartest AI workflow."
2. **Curiosity** — Create an information gap. Viewer must think "How?"
3. **Pain** — Explain the problem.
4. **Demonstration** — Show. Don't explain. Use screen recordings, workflow diagrams, automation, before/after, money, time saved.
5. **Payoff** — Deliver a measurable result. Saved £420. Saved 18 hours. Reduced staff. Tripled leads.
6. **CTA** — Never use generic CTAs. Instead: Comment "WORKFLOW" · Comment "PROMPT" · Comment "AI" · Save this · Send to your business partner.
