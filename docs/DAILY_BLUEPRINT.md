## 2. Production Deliverables

### Per Session — 3 Aesthetic Tiers Captured Simultaneously

**Tier 1 — Editorial (IG Main Feed)**
- 3–4 shots per session
- Camera: 50mm f/2.0 equivalent, natural light
- Lighting: soft window light or golden hour
- Wardrobe: smart casual — structured overshirt, clean t-shirt, neutral colours, gold hoop earrings
- Required flawed realism: visible freckles, visible pores, one stray hair, natural skin texture
- Aspect ratio: 4:5
- Vibe: "She's successful because she's smart, not because she's showing off"

**Tier 2 — Stories / BTS (IG Stories + Tease)**
- 5–8 shots per session
- Camera: 35mm f/1.8 equivalent, handheld
- Lighting: available light, natural, window light only
- Required flawed realism: slightly crooked framing, natural morning light, candid
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical) for stories

**Tier 3 — Raw / Reel (IG Reels + Video)**
- 2–3 shots + 1 short video per session
- Camera: natural perspective, handheld or tripod
- Lighting: whatever exists — mixed indoor/outdoor, natural
- Required flawed realism: slight motion blur, candid framing, unstaged
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

**Video/Reels (1 per session):**
- Duration: 15–45 seconds
- Hook (first 2 seconds): screen showing an AI tool, or hands on keyboard, or a surprising result
- Audio: natural ambient or trending audio; voiceover for tutorials
- Movement: screen recording → cut to Aeloria explaining → cut to result
- End frame: Aeloria looking at camera with a satisfied half-smile
